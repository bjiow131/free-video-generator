# SEASON 1 — PRIVATE SERIES BIBLE
> Working document for production planning. Do not paste episode plots or dialogue into public PR descriptions. This branch is intentionally separate from the character-library branch. It is a development branch, not a confidentiality boundary if the repository itself is public.

## Series concept
Working title: **Мия и тропинка открытий**.
Format: 30 episodes, target runtime 5–10 minutes each. Preschool/family adventure with gentle comedy, nature observation, everyday problem solving, and emotional learning. Each episode is self-contained but contributes to a season-long thread: Mia and Stepa build a "Книга маленьких открытий" by observing nature carefully and learning that helping does not mean controlling or disturbing wildlife.

## Audience and tone
- Audience: roughly 3–7 with co-viewing.
- Tone: warm, curious, visually clear, never frightening; comedy comes from timing, misunderstandings, and physical behavior.
- Each story: a concrete goal, a small obstacle, 2–3 attempts, a discovery, a cooperative solution, and a short observation added to the notebook.
- No dangerous imitation: riding requires helmets and adult supervision; wild animals are observed from a distance; no touching unknown plants/fungi, no feeding wildlife, no leaving the path, no taking animals home.
- No songs or score baked into generated footage. Dialogue/audio/music is layered separately. Dialogue is marked in the scene plan as word-level start/end markers that open/close the speaking character's mouth; no phoneme-level lip-sync system is required.

## Season-long character arcs
- **Мия** begins with enthusiasm and a desire to help immediately. She learns to pause, observe, and ask before acting.
- **Стёпа** is inventive and quick to make a plan, but sometimes rushes. He learns to test ideas safely and revise them.
- **Спираль** the snail is a recurring quiet catalyst. Most of the time it communicates through movement and eye-stalk gestures; when it speaks, lines are short and marked for simple mouth opening/closing.
- **Тишка** values collecting and saving things; learns that not everything found outdoors is a souvenir.
- **Коля** is careful and orderly; learns to try small new things without losing his caution.
- **Пип** gives clues from above, but is not an all-knowing guide.
- **Ква**, **Луна**, and **Рогач** open the pond, pollinator, and woodland-animal story worlds.
- **Мама**, **Папа**, **Бабушка Аня**, and **Лев** appear as adults who scaffold discovery, not solve every child problem for them.

## Core visual rules
- Use one master model and rig per recurring character. Never create per-episode duplicates of hero meshes.
- Keep approved silhouette, height ratios, palette, clothing, shell spiral, markings, and accessories stable.
- A photo is an anatomy/composition reference, not a finished 3D model. Adult human CC0 packs are rig/style references only and must not determine the faces or body proportions of Mia or Stepa.
- Reusable sets: home/yard, forest gate, main path/meadow, grandma's garden, pond, tree hollow, forest clearing, hill viewpoint.
- Props should be stable asset IDs. Yellow scooter and Mia's bicycle are hero continuity props.
- Every spoken line has speaker_id and word-level mouth_open/mouth_close markers. The audio track is added separately.
- First season should prioritize a small number of polished reusable sets over 30 unique environments.

## Episode matrix (internal)
Each entry is a story blueprint, not final dialogue. Target 5–10 minutes, usually 5–7 scenes.

1. **Улитка на тропинке** — Mia wants to race her yellow scooter; she notices Spiral crossing the path and learns to slow down, wait, and help by protecting the route rather than picking the snail up.
2. **Куда пропал след?** — Rain interrupts the visible snail trail. The friends compare damp and dry surfaces and discover why the trail looks different; they record observations rather than inventing a magical explanation.
3. **Самокат и шлем** — Mia is impatient to go downhill. Dad helps her inspect the scooter, put on a helmet, and choose a safe flat route; the payoff is a tiny controlled race with a snail as the slowest judge.
4. **Орех Тишки** — Tishka tries to carry too many nuts and loses track of one. The children learn that wildlife stores food in different places and leave the squirrel's cache undisturbed.
5. **Пип видит сверху** — Pip's calls draw attention to a fork in the path. The children use the map and a landmark instead of blindly following a bird.
6. **Маленькая лужа Квы** — Kva needs a dry route across a shallow muddy edge. The children place no permanent structures; they observe how the frog jumps and keep the bank undisturbed.
7. **Цветок для Луны** — The children want to bring a butterfly closer. Grandma explains how to observe quietly and leave flowers growing; Luna's flight becomes the visual payoff.
8. **Колины иголки** — A leaf sticks to Kolya's spines. The children propose silly solutions before discovering that the hedgehog can move safely and should not be handled.
9. **Велосипедный звонок** — Stepa's bicycle bell startles the animals. They agree on a quiet zone near the forest and practice slowing down before the animal-observation area.
10. **Карта из листьев** — A gust scatters the children's paper map. They reconstruct the route from landmarks and learn that leaves are not reliable labels for every tree.
11. **День дождевых капель** — The group shelter is safe and supervised; they compare raindrop sizes and watch runoff without entering flowing water.
12. **Гриб, который не трогают** — A striking mushroom tempts the children to pick it. Lev teaches a simple rule: observe, sketch, don't touch or eat unknown fungi.
13. **Бублик и потерянная перчатка** — Bublik finds a glove on the path. The children follow the safe lost-property routine and return it to its owner rather than treating it as treasure.
14. **Большое дерево** — They hear a sound in the tree hollow and imagine several explanations. They observe from a distance and discover the sound is wind plus a bird, without reaching into the hollow.
15. **Кто оставил дорожку?** — Tracks and marks in soft ground spark guesses. The children compare size and direction but learn to say "we don't know yet" instead of declaring certainty.
16. **Бабушкин огород** — Mia wants to water every plant at once. Grandma shows how to check soil first; the children use a small watering can and observe which plants need water.
17. **Мостик из веточек** — The group builds a tiny bridge for a toy, not for wildlife, and tests stability safely on a dry patch; the story separates play from changing the real habitat.
18. **Тень на поляне** — Clouds change the shape of shadows. Stepa thinks the forest is moving; the children track the sun and make a simple shadow drawing.
19. **Секрет раковины** — Spiral's shell pattern prompts questions about growth and protection. The friends inspect a photo/reference and draw the spiral, never trying to remove the shell.
20. **Пруд без мусора** — A wrapper is found near the pond. With adult guidance, the children use a bag to collect litter without entering the water and learn to leave the place cleaner.
21. **Рогач на расстоянии** — A young deer appears briefly at the forest edge. The children stay still and quiet; the moment teaches respectful distance rather than pursuit.
22. **Шишка-почтальон** — A pine cone is used as a pretend message token during a game. They avoid placing objects in animal burrows and return the cone where it was found.
23. **Тихий час леса** — The children try to hear the forest's smallest sounds. Their own movement keeps interrupting; they practice stopping, listening, and taking turns.
24. **Куда едет вода?** — They trace rainwater from the hill to the pond using a small controlled water demonstration at home, then compare it with what they can safely observe outside.
25. **Праздник маленьких открытий** — The friends plan a mini-exhibition of drawings and notes. Tishka wants to display real finds, but the group chooses sketches and photos instead.
26. **Потерянная страница** — A notebook page blows away. The group remembers observations and recreates the page together, showing that collaboration can recover work without pretending it is identical.
27. **День без спешки** — Mia plans too many activities in one outing. They pick one small observation and discover more by slowing down.
28. **Новая тропинка?** — A tempting unofficial shortcut appears. Lev explains why the group stays on the marked path; they find a safe viewpoint without trampling plants.
29. **Книга открытий** — The group organizes 29 notes into categories and notices connections between weather, tracks, plants, and animal behavior. Each character contributes one strength.
30. **Тропа продолжается** — At the season finale, the group shares the completed "Книгу маленьких открытий" with family. Spiral crosses the path again; this time everyone pauses naturally, demonstrating the changed behavior. The final image opens the possibility of new discoveries without a cliffhanger.

## Production pipeline gates
1. Lock visual style and reference boards.
2. Approve hero silhouettes and turnaround sheets.
3. Build and validate hero rigs, including simple mouth-open controls.
4. Validate reusable sets and hero props.
5. Produce a 20–30 second pilot animatic before scaling to full episode runtime.
6. For each shot, store scene_id, asset_ids, camera framing, dialogue speaker IDs, word-level mouth markers, duration, and action beats.
7. Render low-resolution previews, check continuity, then final output.
8. Track source URLs, licenses, retrieval date, hashes, and local asset paths.
