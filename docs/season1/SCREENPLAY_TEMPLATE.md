# Season 1 screenplay template

Use this template for each episode in the private production workspace. Do not put confidential episode plots in the public character-library repository.

## Episode metadata
- Episode number and title
- Story goal and learning takeaway
- Continuity dependencies
- Target runtime: 300–600 seconds
- Status: outline / scripted / shot-planned / animatic / rendered / approved

## Scene block
- Scene ID and location ID
- Story purpose and emotional turn
- Estimated duration and transitions
- Characters and stable asset IDs
- Physical action, reactions, pauses, and safe staging
- Dialogue: speaker ID, exact line, estimated word timing
- Camera: shot type, target, aspect ratio (9:16 / 16:9 / 1:1), movement, framing, cut point
- Props and continuity state at scene start/end
- Required reusable effects
- Production notes and unresolved blockers

## Dialogue timing record
For each spoken line, create word spans with start_seconds and end_seconds, plus mouth open/close events. Mark timing source as script_estimate until aligned to final audio. Keep audio/music separate. Do not attempt phoneme-level sync.

## Runtime acceptance
- Sum scene/shot duration and estimate spoken runtime.
- Verify total duration is 300–600 seconds.
- Check setup, complication, attempts, discovery, resolution, and emotional ending.
- Check continuity with previous episodes and reuse of master characters, locations, and props.
- A beat outline is not a finished screenplay.
